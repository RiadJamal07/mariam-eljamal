FAQ

Got any questions?

How does an engagement start?

With a free 30-minute call. You describe the problem in whatever state it's in — half-formed is fine — and we sketch a first path forward together. No stress, no commitment.

What tools do you work with?

Python, R and SQL for analysis; Power BI, Tableau and Excel for dashboards; KoboToolbox and SurveyCTO for data collection; SharePoint and Databricks on the platform side.

What sectors do you know best?

International development (UN agencies, NGOs, donor reporting), health and epidemiology research, and academic institutions — with recent work in finance through the LSE program.

Do you work remotely?

Yes — I work with teams remotely and on the ground, and I've led field operations end-to-end when a project needed it.

Can you train our team, not just deliver?

That's the point. I'd rather leave your team confident with their own data than have you depend on me forever. Workshops and hands-on training are part of most engagements.

Something else?

Just reach out — moe13@mail.aub.edu. Bring the messy version.
